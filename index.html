About Me

Who am I?

For over a decade, I've built my career as a higher education professional; an academic, life, and career coach; and a program manager focused on developing and maintaining systems that help people navigate complex environments thoughtfully and with clear intent.


My work has spanned graduate education, academic advising, and program administration, supporting students throughout the entire student lifecycle, from recruitment through graduation. Along the way, I've developed a particular interest in the infrastructure that makes effective student support possible. Effective data management and reporting, careful technological integration, continual process improvement, and ongoing resource development are vital to the success of administrative and advising professionals, and thus, for the success of their students.


No matter where my work takes me, I tend to gravitate toward opportunities that allow me to explore the intersections of people, systems, and information. I enjoy translating complicated policies, institutional needs, and technical possibilities into clear, practical solutions that work for people, not against them. I am especially interested in how thoughtful system design, collaborative problem-solving, and the effective use of technology can strengthen operations while improving the experiences both of students and of the professionals who support them.


I am particularly energized by work that combines careful relationship-building with thoughtful problem-solving. Whether I am advising a student, coaching a professional, developing new processes or technological solutions, facilitating workshops, or rethinking established practices, I approach my work with curiosity, creativity, and a commitment to building sustainable, meaningful experiences for all involved.
